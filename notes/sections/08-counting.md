# S08 — Counting

> [!NOTE]
> Discrete Math, section 08. Index: [Discrete Math Sections](../section-plan.md) · questions: [Discrete Math Question Bank](../../practice/question-bank.md) · teaching notes: [Discrete Math Playbook](../teaching-playbook.md).

**One idea to land:** **choose the model before you compute.** Ordered or not? Repetition allowed or not? Everything else is arithmetic.

**Board:**
1. The decision tree: order matters + no repetition → permutation; order irrelevant → combination; repetition allowed → powers or stars-and-bars.
2. Sum vs product rule on a real problem, and where students always double count.
3. Inclusion–exclusion on two then three sets, with the Venn picture beside it.
4. Pigeonhole, and one non-obvious application.
5. Verify every answer on a small case by brute-force listing — the habit that saves them in the exam.

**They count:** committee problems (with "at least one" forcing complement counting), passwords with constraints, and identical-vs-distinct objects.

**Mistakes I expect:** permutation where combination was meant; "at least one" counted directly instead of by complement; double counting in inclusion–exclusion; treating identical objects as distinct.

**Close:** "Count it twice. Two derivations agreeing beats one derivation you feel good about."
